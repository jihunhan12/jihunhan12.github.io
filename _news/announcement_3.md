Prof. Jihun Han attended the 2026 IFAC World Congress in Busan from August 23 to 28. More than 4,000 participants from over 70 countries registered for the congress. He gave three presentations: one in a technical session and two in industrial group forums, including a joint workshop with Hyundai Motor Company.
