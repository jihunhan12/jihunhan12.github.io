>Enable safe, user-centric, and efficient mobility through cooperative intelligence and control across users, vehicles, and infrastructure

## Research Areas

### Big data
- Data collection from various sources (such as vehicles, users, drones, and infrastructure)
- Scalable data pipelines and processing frameworks
- Data analysis

### Modeling & Simulation
- Powertrain and vehicle dynamics modeling
- Human driver and interaction modeling
- Automated vehicle system modeling, from Level 1-2 driver assistance to Level 3-5 automated driving
- Multi-scale simulation across vehicle, corridor, and network levels
- Scenario generation, calibration, and evaluation

### Intelligence & Control
- Heterogeneous sensing and sensor fusion
- Prediction of driving behaviors, queues, and traffic flow
- User-centric control across powertrain, vehicle, and network levels
- Cooperative control through vehicle-infrastructure interaction

### Experimental Verification & Validation
- Scaled automated vehicle testing
- Simulation-to-XIL-to-road testing workflow
- X-in-the-loop testing, including vehicle, driver, V2X, etc.
- Track and public road testing
